Audioria Local Voice bundles, verifies, and adapts the reviewed browser-runtime
snapshot from KevinAHM/pocket-tts-web under public/local-voice/vendor. The
upstream browser integration is unofficial; its
source code is Apache License 2.0 (a full copy is bundled as CODE-LICENSE.txt).
Its current ONNX export separately declares CC BY-NC 4.0 in onnx/ONNX-LICENSE, and its bundled
voice catalog mixes CC0, CC BY 4.0, and CC BY-NC 4.0 sources. For that reason,
this exact local engine is limited to Audioria's free, non-commercial evidence
preview and must not be used as the engine of a paid plan.

Pocket TTS's official code is MIT and its official model weights are CC BY 4.0.
A commercial Audioria release requires a reproducible export from those
official weights plus an independently cleared voice pack, or a separately
licensed cloud engine. The built-in catalog from this community bundle is not
exposed as a commercial preset.
